Low Salience

Keep WhatsApp Web open. Leave the distractions out.

A browser extension for people who work in WhatsApp Web all day. It hides the parts built to grab your attention, so you deal with the conversations you choose, when you choose.

Install: Chrome Web Store · Firefox Add-ons · Microsoft Edge Add-ons

What it hides

  • Status: the Status button and its update dot
  • Channels: the Channels button
  • Communities: the Communities button
  • Archived: the Archived row and its counter
  • Unread badges: the green counters on chats, on the side bar and on label filters, and the green time of new messages
  • Tab counter: the “(3)” in the browser tab title and the number on the tab icon

Private and fast by design

Low Salience collects no data, makes no network requests and never reads your messages, contacts or media. It hides elements with a stylesheet the browser applies before WhatsApp draws the page, and its small script only looks at the side bar and the tab title, never at your chat list. Read the privacy policy.

Help

How do I pause it?

Click the Low Salience icon in the toolbar and turn off the switch at the top, or press Alt+Shift+F. Everything comes back instantly; press again to hide it all.

Can I hide only some things?

Yes. Each item in the popup has its own switch. Changes apply immediately.

Something is showing again

WhatsApp changes its interface from time to time, which can stop an item from being hidden until we ship an update. Reload WhatsApp Web first; if it is still visible, tell us which item and your browser (contact below) and we will fix it.

The keyboard shortcut does not work

Another extension may use the same keys. Change it at chrome://extensions/shortcuts (Chrome, Edge, Brave) or in Firefox under Add-ons → ⚙ → Manage Extension Shortcuts.

Does it work with WhatsApp Business?

Yes, on WhatsApp Web (web.whatsapp.com) for both regular and Business accounts.

Does it change my account or my phone?

No. It only hides parts of the screen in your browser. Your chats, archive, status and notifications on your phone are untouched. Uninstall it and WhatsApp Web looks exactly as before.
