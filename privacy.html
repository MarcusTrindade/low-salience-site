Low Salience

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-16

Low Salience does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

What the extension does

It hides parts of the WhatsApp Web interface (such as Status, Channels and unread badges) by applying a stylesheet to https://web.whatsapp.com. It does not read, store or send your messages, contacts, chats or media, and it does not interact with WhatsApp's code.

What is stored

Only your preferences: which items are hidden and whether focus mode is on. They are kept in your browser's extension storage and, if you use browser sync, synced by your browser between your own devices. They never reach us.

Network

The extension makes no network requests. There are no analytics, trackers, ads or third-party services.

Permissions

storage to remember your preferences, access to web.whatsapp.com to hide interface elements there, and scripting to re-apply itself to WhatsApp Web tabs that were already open when the extension is updated or turned back on. Nothing else.

Changes

If a future version adds a paid plan, license validation will be described here before it ships, and will only send what is required to validate the license. The date at the top of this page shows the latest change.
